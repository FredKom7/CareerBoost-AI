AI Prediction & Matching Engine

Discover the engineering and data science behind our high-fidelity resume skill scoring and matching algorithms.

Gemini Semantic Evaluation

Unlike standard exact-match scrapers that fail on syntax variations, our integration with Gemini NLP models conducts semantic skill evaluation.

  • Synonym Mapping: Recognizes that "Postgres" matches "SQL" and "ReactJS" implies "React".
  • Contextual Understanding: Evaluates experience depth based on surrounding phrasing rather than mere keyword presence.
  • Acronym Decoding: Correctly maps shorthand terms to standardized technical categories.

ATS Scoring & Fallback Architecture

We combine advanced remote neural nets with lightning-fast, secure local fallback matchers to ensure constant availability and accuracy.

  • Weighted Matching: Calculates the final score by comparing candidate skill density against benchmark company requirements.
  • Local Substring Scanners: Triggers seamless fallback scanners instantly if the main NLP model encounters rate limits or downtime.
  • Benchmarked Comparisons: Ranks your final ATS score against real applicant medians and elite (Top 10%) performance targets.
